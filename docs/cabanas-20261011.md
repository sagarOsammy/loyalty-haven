## Placement and collision fit

The final local layout fits cabanas uniformly within 4.3 by 2.45 by 3.7 metres, at boardwalk Y 0.05. Centers are (-46, -13.2), (-44.5, -17.8), (-5.05, -13.2), (25, -13.2), (35.4, -13.2), and (41.3, -13.2). The staggered western pair and inner western cabana improve visibility without moving buildings or the camera. Part of the cabana near X 25 remains concealed by the hotel.

The final audit checks actual existing planter bounds, all palm wells, leaf beds, 24 path lamps, the central avenue, boardwalk limits and cabana-to-cabana keepouts. Runtime reports zero conflicts. The final layout supersedes the preliminary 5 by 2.8 metre row, whose cabanas were partly obscured and two overlapped existing planters. No existing pots or lamps were moved.

## Import adapter and transformation

`WaterfrontCabanas` creates six named anchors and accepts a caller-supplied `CabanaAssetLoader` callback. `inspectCabanaAsset` records mesh, vertex, triangle and bound counts before repetition. `load` accepts the source only when the inspected triangle budget is at most 80,000, or when the parent supplies explicit derivative verification. It then clones the inspected source six times, clones materials per instance, preserves any source root scale while fitting each copy to its anchor, grounds it to the boardwalk, and fits it within 4.3 by 2.45 by 3.7 metres. Optional reference material hints identify frame, canopy, curtain and daybed meshes without forcing source material changes during inspection. Reload and clear dispose only the cloned material objects, leaving shared source textures and geometry owned by the source loader. A disposed group rejects late loader resolution before any clone can attach.

The intended source components are teak frame and posts, white canopy, tied white curtains and cream upholstered daybed. The supplied source is merged, so roles are recorded and the canopy roof receives a separate material, while the runtime mesh remains one economical mesh. The original source remains preserved beside the derivative, with the local source hash and transformations recorded in `asset-sources/cabanas/inventory.json`.
