# Outdoor benches, stage 8

This folder records the selected compact seating candidate for the eighth scene change. The reference uses timber benches or compact outdoor sofas with cream cushions, placed beside planting beds at the visible foreground edges and around the forum while keeping the central avenue open.

The selected source is the original Tripo Option A candidate, [wooden bench with beige upholstered cushion and solid wood frame](https://studio.tripo3d.ai/3d-model/wooden-bench-with-beige-upholstered-cushion-and-solid-wood-frame-6a88819d-bbd3-4333-af71-f7aafb1fee7b). It is selected for the timber frame, beige cushion, compact footprint and expected repeatability. The supplied export is preserved byte for byte at `source/bench.glb`, SHA-256 `c70555b312b041ad52ea3aaa50530aa63e488d8f6f7dd977813f1847cf0a5007`, 2,881,820 bytes.

The Tripo page reported 594,374 faces or triangle-topology elements and 306,371 vertices on 11 October 2026, and the local export confirms those counts. It contains one 2048 by 2048 packed color map and one material. The separate runtime derivative is `public/assets/outdoor-bench-tripo.glb`, SHA-256 `7c1864810f629a81532f400f85d8e39e238badee000e7b868e2b3901cf7e3791`, 1,295,480 bytes. It contains 19,999 triangles, 15,328 mesh vertices before export and 33,544 vertices after glTF re-import with UV seam expansion, plus one packed 1024 by 1024 sRGB JPEG color map exported at quality 90. Both vertices and triangles are now recorded before repeating the asset four times.

The final glTF Y-up bounds are 3.050381 metres wide, 0.987933 metres high and 0.84752 metres deep. The source axes are converted through Blender's Z-up export space as Blender X=source Y, Blender Y=-source X, Blender Z=source Z, producing final glTF X=source Y, glTF Y=source Z and glTF Z=source X. This keeps the cushion horizontal, grounded at glTF Y 0, with the cushion-facing side at glTF +Z.

The isolated adapter in `lib/scene/outdoor-benches.ts` accepts a caller supplied loader, fits each copy within a 3.05 by 1.12 by 1.25 metre envelope, grounds the source at Y 0.03, preserves authored root transforms, shares one owned cloned material set across the four instances and leaves shared geometry and maps owned by the caller's asset pool. It applies an 80,000 triangle and 100,000 vertex repeated-instance gate unless the parent records an explicit optimization review. The prepared derivative passes that gate. It deliberately has no procedural fallback when the selected source cannot be loaded.

The four reserved seating pockets are authored in `lib/scene/bench-layout.ts`. Two sit at the west and east foreground edges, and two frame the north side of the forum. The exported keepouts let the flowering tree and parasol passes avoid the bench footprints while reserving their own seating zones.
